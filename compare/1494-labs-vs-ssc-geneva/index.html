1494 Labs vs SS&C Geneva

A fund accounting comparison for a hedge fund team reviewing its investment book and investor accounting. Use a worked close to test the fit.

Product sources reviewed November 3, 2026

Start with the accounting workflow

SS&C Advent describes Geneva as a platform for portfolio and fund management, investor accounting and reporting, and shadow accounting. Source: SS&C Advent Geneva product page, reviewed November 3, 2026.

1494 Labs configures fees, allocations and waterfalls to the fund’s agreement. Investment records and investor capital accounts meet in one ledger, with recurring checks and a trail from a reported balance to its entries. See the capability matrix for current product scope.

Reconcile the investment book and investor result

Use a representative period with a trade, a valuation change, income and an investor capital movement. Reconcile positions and cash to the ledger, then follow income and expenses into the investor allocation. Compare the book with an independent administrator statement and investigate one deliberate difference.

  1. Agree the opening book, governing terms and expected results before the demonstration.
  2. Run the same dated transactions and valuations in each evaluation.
  3. Compare fees, investor allocations and closing balances, with an explanation for every difference.
  4. Ask to follow a reported amount back through its calculation and ledger entries.

Take the expected results away with the calculation evidence. The review should show whether the proposed setup handles your fund’s terms and whether your team can explain the close.
