An alternative to LemonEdge: fund accounting with 1494 Labs

Evaluate 1494 Labs for a fund team working through multi-level ownership and allocation rules, with configured terms, ledger traces and a worked acceptance case.

Product sources reviewed November 3, 2026

Put your fund’s close at the center of the shortlist

LemonEdge describes fund accounting for complex private equity structures, with event-based partnership accounting and allocation paths for ownership, carry and waterfalls. Its published scope is described on the LemonEdge private equity product page, reviewed November 3, 2026.

If you are considering alternatives, start with the work the new book must reproduce: the investment record, the fee terms, each investor’s allocation and the statement the controller approves. 1494 Labs brings those records onto one ledger and runs recurring checks that surface differences.

Follow an allocation through the ownership structure

Model the actual ownership chain with a feeder and underlying vehicle. Post income and an expense in the underlying book, then follow their allocation to the feeder’s investors. Inspect the ownership basis, currency treatment and ledger entries. Reconcile the combined view to the individual books.

Build a review pack before the demo

  • The opening position: an agreed ledger, investment positions and investor capital balances.
  • The terms: the fee rate and base, allocation rules, waterfall provisions and any investor-specific arrangements.
  • The state: relevant high-water marks, loss carryforwards, equalization balances and side-pocket interests.
  • The activity: dated transactions and valuations for one representative period.
  • The expected evidence: reconciled fees, investor allocations, ledger entries and ending balances.

What to ask 1494 Labs to show

Ask to configure the terms, run the period and trace the output from the investor statement back to its source. Then introduce a correction in an open period and review the changed calculation and entries. Inspect an exception from the checks and the information a controller uses to resolve it.

Use reference data shaped like your fund for the first evaluation. Agree the scope of imports and any mid-life conversion before choosing a cutover date. The administrator conversion checklist sets out an acceptance process.
