# 1494 Labs vs FIS Private Capital Suite

A fund accounting comparison for a private capital team evaluating fund accounting, carry and investor reporting. Use a worked close to test the fit.

Product sources reviewed November 3, 2026

## Start with the accounting workflow

FIS Private Capital Suite, formerly Investran, is described by FIS as a platform for fund and partnership accounting and automated investor reporting. Source: [FIS Private Capital Suite product page](https://www.fisglobal.com/products/fis-private-capital-suite), reviewed November 3, 2026.

1494 Labs configures fees, allocations and waterfalls to the fund’s agreement. Investment records and investor capital accounts meet in one ledger, with recurring checks and a trail from a reported balance to its entries. See the [capability matrix](https://1494labs.com/capabilities/) for current product scope.

## Compare waterfall results under the fund’s agreement

Bring two exits with different dates and a later capital call. Specify whether the waterfall is whole-of-fund or deal-by-deal, and agree the preferred-return and catch-up terms. Follow the LP and GP amounts through the calculation and ledger. Add a dated correction and explain the change in the result.

1. Agree the opening book, governing terms and expected results before the demonstration.

2. Run the same dated transactions and valuations in each evaluation.

3. Compare fees, investor allocations and closing balances, with an explanation for every difference.

4. Ask to follow a reported amount back through its calculation and ledger entries.

Take the expected results away with the calculation evidence. The review should show whether the proposed setup handles your fund’s terms and whether your team can explain the close.
