Capital Call and Distribution Software

For fund managers, allocators and administrators tracking investor commitments and cash flows.

Accounting Capabilities

  • Investor bank details have a change-and-approval workflow. Account details are masked in the investor screen; revealing them requires permission. Approved details supply the bank information used in notices.
  • Capital call notices are available as PDFs through the API. The notice carries the investor's amount, due date and wire instructions from approved bank details, for the accounting team's call-notice process.
  • Distribution notices are available as PDFs through the API. Each notice belongs to a recorded distribution and provides the investor-facing document for that cash event.
  • Capital Accounts shows unfunded commitments by investor and fund. A fund setting chooses the historical or spot measure, so the reported unfunded amount follows the accounting basis selected for that fund.
  • Capital Accounts shows investor balances for the selected fund. Accounting checks compare investor capital snapshots with general ledger capital, giving the investor balances a defined tie to the fund's capital accounts.
  • Investor statements show opening capital, contributions, distributions, allocated income and expenses, fees and ending capital. The statement draws these amounts from the investor's recorded account and the fund's reporting period.

Terms

Commitment budget, called capital and unfunded commitment describe an investor's funding position. Recallable capital and distributions recycled describe returned amounts that the fund terms permit to be called again.

Questions

Can I retrieve a capital call notice through the API?

Yes. The capital call notice endpoint returns a PDF with the investor's amount, due date and wire instructions. Wire instructions come from approved bank details. The notice is retrieved through the API.

Can I retrieve an investor distribution notice?

Yes. The distribution notice endpoint returns the PDF for a recorded distribution. It provides the investor-facing notice for that distribution through the API.

Can I see unfunded commitments by investor?

Yes. Capital Accounts reports unfunded commitments per investor and per fund. The fund's setting selects the historical or spot measure used in that view, giving the team a defined basis for the outstanding amount.

Are changes to investor bank details approved?

Yes. Bank-detail changes require a second person's approval. The investor screen masks the stored details, and revealing them requires a separate permission. Notices use the approved bank details.

Do investor capital accounts tie to general ledger capital?

Yes. The accounting checks compare investor capital balances with the fund's general ledger capital. Capital Accounts displays the investor balances. The tie is an accounting invariant checked across the book.
