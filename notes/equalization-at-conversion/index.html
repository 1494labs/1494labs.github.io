Equalization at conversion: check each subscriber separately

Two investors can hold the same number of shares and need different fee outcomes. Test the imported records investor by investor.

Fund operations · November 3, 2026

Preserve the method as well as the balance

Record whether the fund uses series accounting or a single NAV with investor-level equalization adjustments. Preserve the relevant subscription dates, quantities, fee rates, marks, outstanding credits or deficits, and settlement history. Confirm whether each reported balance is before or after accrued fees and equalization adjustments.

Do not translate a credit into a high-water mark, or merge series, merely because two opening NAV totals agree. The governing terms determine the method and the records needed to continue it.

  1. Agree the method and crystallization dates with the controller and the outgoing provider.
  2. Reconcile each investor's quantity, opening value and fee-state record to the source schedule.
  3. Identify subscribers who entered above the existing mark, below it and at it.
  4. Run recovery, further-gain and reversal cases under the same agreed assumptions in both books.
  5. Compare each investor's fee and closing economics, then reconcile the total to the ledger.
  6. Test a redemption and any pending equalization settlement before accepting the setup.

A recovery case that separates the investors

Assume a 20% incentive fee on gains above each investor's own mark, no hurdle, no other fees and no intervening capital activity. A subscribed at $100; B subscribed after a decline at $80. Each holds 10,000 shares. The test ends at a gross, pre-incentive-fee value of $100 per share.

InvestorOwn markGross test valueGain above own markExpected fee
A$100$1,000,000$0$0
B$80$1,000,000$200,000$40,000

Separate series can carry those marks directly. A single-NAV method needs the appropriate investor adjustments to reach the agreed economics. Compare the resulting investor values and fee allocations; do not require identical journal lines from methods with different mechanics.

Investigate a total that ties

A fund-level reconciliation does not prove that the fee was charged to the correct investors. Trace any difference through the subscription price, imported fee state, adjustment postings and settlement. Retain the original state and the expected test result alongside the conversion evidence.
