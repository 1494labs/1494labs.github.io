# Side pockets: keep the investment and its investors together

A side pocket needs its own participation record. A later subscriber should not silently acquire an interest in an earlier illiquid investment.

Fund operations · November 3, 2026

## Start with who participates

A side pocket separates an investment from the main pool for accounting and investor participation. The fund documents determine who participates, how it is valued and when proceeds can be distributed. A separate security label alone does not preserve those terms.

1. Identify the investment and the effective date of the side pocket.

2. Record the participating investors and their interests on that date.

3. Keep subsequent subscriptions and redemptions in the main pool from changing that participation unless the governing terms require it.

4. Allocate the side pocket's valuation changes, income and expenses to its participants.

5. On realization, reconcile proceeds and remaining balances to those same investors.

## A small example

Assume an investment worth $1 million is placed in a side pocket when investor A owns 60% and investor B owns 40% of the participating capital. Investor C subscribes to the main pool later. Under terms that fix side-pocket participation at designation, C has no interest in this investment.

| Investor | Opening side-pocket interest | Share of a $100,000 gain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | $600,000 | $60,000 |
| B | $400,000 | $40,000 |
| C | $0 | $0 |
| Total | $1,000,000 | $100,000 |

The example excludes fees and cash flows. Those need their own treatment under the fund's terms; they should not be inferred from the gain allocation.

## What the controller checks

Reconcile the investment's carrying value to the sum of its investor interests. Then check the participation record independently. A total that ties can still allocate money to the wrong investors. Test a subscription after designation, a partial redemption and a realization before relying on the setup.

On conversion, carry the side-pocket participation record with the opening values. Rebuilding interests from today's main-pool percentages can change the economics while leaving total NAV unchanged.
