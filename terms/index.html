Using this site

Four things worth stating plainly, because the notes on this site are written to be acted on.

Last reviewed 2026-09-16

The notes are not advice

The notes work through fund accounting mechanics with real numbers, and they are written for people who do this for a living. They are not accounting, legal, tax or investment advice, and reading them creates no professional relationship between us.

Fund terms vary in ways that change the answer. A high-water mark prorates on partial redemption because that is what the arithmetic requires, but whether it does in your fund is a question for your offering document, not for us. Where a note works an example, the example is the example. It is not your fund.

The screenshots are synthetic

Every figure, fund and investor name in a screenshot on this site is invented for illustration. The books behind them are generated, the numbers are internally consistent because the platform computed them, and none of it is any real firm's data. No customer's book has ever been photographed for this site, and none will be.

Published prices are what we charge, not a contract

The prices on the pricing page are real and we will honour one you saw for a reasonable period. They are not an offer capable of acceptance on their own. A subscription begins with a written agreement, which is provided with a proposal rather than published, and that agreement is what governs use of the platform.

Accuracy, and what to send us

We take care to be right and we correct what we find to be wrong, but we do not warrant that everything here is accurate, complete or current. Capabilities and prices described on this site may change.

What you send through the enquiry form or by email is handled as described in the privacy notice, and reaches the third parties named on the sub-processors page. Please do not send confidential information, personal data about your investors, or anything covered by an obligation of confidence, before there is an agreement in place that covers it.

Our content

The text, images and design of this site are ours. Quote from the notes and link to them freely. If you want to reproduce a whole note rather than quote from it, ask; the answer is usually yes.
