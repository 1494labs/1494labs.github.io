What we collect, and what we deliberately do not

There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to. This page is the inventory.

Last reviewed 2026-10-18

Who we are

This site, the documentation site and the application are operated by 1494 Labs Inc., a Delaware corporation, 6 Liberty Square #2721, Boston, MA 02109. For anything on this page, write to hello@1494labs.com. For the personal data described here we are the controller; for data a customer places in its workspace, the customer is the controller and we process it on its instructions. We are in the United States, and what reaches us is stored and processed there.

This website

1494labs.com sets no cookies. It runs one analytics tool, Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is the reason this section changed on the date above. It stores nothing on your device: no cookie, no local storage, no fingerprint, and no identifier that would let us recognize you on a second visit or follow you to another site. What it records is a count: which page was requested, which site referred it, the country the request came from, and how long the page took to load. We can tell that forty people read a note and that most of them arrived from LinkedIn. We cannot tell that you were one of them, and we have deliberately chosen a tool that cannot tell us.

The site is served through Cloudflare, which may inject a small script that hides our email address from spam harvesters and, under attack conditions, may set a short-lived security cookie of its own; both are protective functions, not tracking. The only other scripts on any page are structured-data metadata for search engines, which do not execute.

The enquiry form on the home page posts what you type directly to Formspree, a form-handling service in the United States, which forwards it to us by email. Formspree receives what you enter and the network address you send it from. Nothing is sent to them unless you submit the form.

The documentation site

docs.1494labs.com sets no cookies. It carries no analytics beacon; the Cloudflare Web Analytics counter described above runs on 1494labs.com only. If you switch its color theme, that preference is stored in your own browser's local storage and never leaves it. The feedback form sends exactly what you type to us, along with the page you sent it from, and nothing else.

The application

app.1494labs.com is the product itself, used under an agreement with the customer whose workspace you sign into. Signing in sets three cookies, all strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to protect your session: a session token, a refresh token, and a cross-site request-forgery token. Signing in through your firm's own identity provider sets one more, short-lived cookie that ties the sign-in request to its answer, and removes it when sign-in completes. They exist only for authentication, are scoped to the application, and end when you sign out or when their fixed lifetime runs out. Interface preferences - theme, table density - live in your browser's local storage. The application loads no external fonts, no advertising code and nothing that follows you to another site. It carries no analytics beacon from the network edge. The Cloudflare Web Analytics counter described above runs on this website only, installed by us, and stores nothing on your device. It never runs inside the application, so no page, fund, investor or figure from a workspace ever reaches it.

The application also sends product analytics to PostHog (United States), so we can see which screens are used and where errors happen. What it sends is narrow and fixed: which screen was opened, recorded as the screen's template - "/funds/{fund}", never the fund itself or anything shown on it - the kind of any error, and a pseudonymous code for you and your workspace, a one-way hash rather than a name or an email address, plus a random identifier for the visit that lives only in the open tab. PostHog also receives your browser type, as every web request carries it. It does not receive your network address: our network edge removes it before anything is forwarded. Nothing is stored on your device - no cookie and no local-storage identifier - and no clicks, keystrokes or screen recordings are captured. No fund, investor or figure ever reaches it.

For security, the application keeps an immutable record of authentication events (sign-ins, failures, credential changes) including the network address they came from. That record exists to protect the books and the accounts, and it is visible to your workspace's administrators.

What reaches us at all

Your email and what you write, if you write to us. Your feedback text, if you submit it. Server access logs - network address, browser identification - kept for 400 days for security and audit. Data you or your firm place in the application belongs to your firm and is processed only to run the service.

Who touches the infrastructure

Amazon Web Services hosts the application and its database (United States). Cloudflare provides the network edge and DDoS protection, and so carries the application's traffic in transit. GitHub hosts this website. Formspree handles the enquiry form on the home page, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page requests on this website only, and PostHog receives the application's product analytics described above. Every one of them, and what reaches it, is listed on the sub-processors page. If a workspace administrator turns on the AI-assisted features, the documents, import samples and support requests sent through them go to Anthropic (or, where one is configured, OpenAI) for that purpose and no other. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We disclose it only to the providers named on the sub-processors page, to run these services, or where the law requires us to.

Why we process it, and for how long

We use what you send us to answer you and, if you ask, to arrange a demonstration: that is a step you have asked us to take, or our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. We keep enquiry emails for up to two years after our last exchange, unless the enquiry becomes a customer relationship. Server access logs and the authentication record exist for our legitimate interest in keeping the services and the books secure; the access log is kept for 400 days. Page counts and product analytics serve our legitimate interest in knowing which pages and screens are used and where errors happen, and identify no one.

Your rights

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants you rights over your personal data - access, correction, deletion, portability, objection - write to hello@1494labs.com and we will respond as that law requires. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may also complain to your data protection authority. For data inside a customer's workspace, we act on the instructions of that customer, who is the controller of it, and we will pass your request to them.

If our practices change in a way that matters - an analytics tool, a new sub-processor - this page changes first, and the date above moves.
