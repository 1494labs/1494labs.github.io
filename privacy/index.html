What we collect, and what we deliberately do not

There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to. This page is the inventory.

Last reviewed 2026-08-30

This website

1494labs.com sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts. The only scripts on any page are structured-data metadata for search engines, which do not execute. The site is served through Cloudflare, which may inject a small script that hides our email address from spam harvesters and, under attack conditions, may set a short-lived security cookie of its own; both are protective functions, not tracking. We do not know who visits this site, and we have chosen not to know.

The documentation site

docs.1494labs.com sets no cookies. If you switch its color theme, that preference is stored in your own browser's local storage and never leaves it. The feedback form sends exactly what you type to us, along with the page you sent it from, and nothing else.

The application

app.1494labs.com is the product itself, used under an agreement with the customer whose workspace you sign into. Signing in sets exactly three cookies, all strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to protect your session: a session token, a refresh token, and a cross-site request-forgery token. They exist only for authentication, are scoped to the application, and expire with your session. Interface preferences - theme, table density - live in your browser's local storage. The application loads nothing from third parties: no analytics, no trackers, no external fonts or scripts.

For security, the application keeps an immutable record of authentication events (sign-ins, failures, credential changes) including the network address they came from. That record exists to protect the books and the accounts, and it is visible to your workspace's administrators.

What reaches us at all

Your email and what you write, if you write to us. Your feedback text, if you submit it. Standard, short-lived server logs - network address, browser identification - kept for security and operations. Data you or your firm place in the application belongs to your firm and is processed only to run the service.

Who touches the infrastructure

Amazon Web Services hosts the application and its database (United States). Cloudflare provides the network edge and DDoS protection. GitHub hosts this website. If a customer enables the AI-assisted features, documents they choose to process are sent to Anthropic for that purpose and no other. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone, for anything.

Your rights

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants you rights over your personal data - access, correction, deletion, portability - write to hello@1494labs.com and we will honor them. For data inside a customer's workspace, we act on the instructions of that customer, who is the controller of it.

If our practices change in a way that matters - an analytics tool, a new sub-processor - this page changes first, and the date above moves.
