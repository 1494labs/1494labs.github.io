What counts as a fund

A master and three feeders is four entities and one economic book, so billing it as four funds would be wrong. Master-feeder, parallel funds, feeders, SPVs and blockers are quoted rather than counted. Share classes, series and sleeves inside a fund are not separate funds: a fund with seventeen series is one fund.
