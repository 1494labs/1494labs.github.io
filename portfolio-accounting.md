# Portfolio Accounting Across Asset Classes

For fund managers, allocators and administrators accounting for multi-asset portfolios.

## Accounting Capabilities

- Valuations records dated marks for underlying fund interests, portfolio companies and priced instruments. The entry fields follow the asset: fund NAV statements carry an as-of date and source; company marks carry a valuation method and memo.

- Fund Statements provides an income statement, balance sheet, statement of changes in net assets and cash-flow statement for one fund and period. The reporting screen uses the selected fund and reporting period.

- Investor statements show opening capital, contributions, distributions, allocated income and expenses, fees and ending capital. The statement draws these amounts from the investor's recorded account and the fund's reporting period.

- Reporting includes report packs organized by audience. A pack runs for its fund and period and can be exported as PDF or CSV, collecting the selected reports for the intended recipient.

- Company Financials & KPIs provides templates for revenue, EBITDA, net debt and custom KPIs. The team can upload a completed template or create a secure submission link for the company. Accepted figures appear beside the company valuation mark, with the submission used recorded.

- The template includes the reporting date and all required metrics. Upload checks cover the expected columns, missing fields, valid dates, duplicate dates and finite numeric values. Revenue, EBITDA and net debt use the company's currency; custom KPIs retain their agreed units.

- Each submission and correction retains its date, currency, source and submitter. The collection history preserves earlier evidence. Operator review accepts or rejects an external submission with a recorded reason.

- The valuation screen shows the collected figures beside the mark and records the financial submission selected for that valuation. Fund report sections include company financials and KPIs and flag companies with missing reports.

- The company Exit screen calculates whole-holding exit proceeds through liquidation preferences, participation and caps. The calculation uses the company's capital structure to allocate the exit value across the security classes.

- The company valuation screen records a dated mark up or down. The company's valuation method and memo stay with the mark, giving quarter-end company valuation records a date and stated calculation approach.

- Underlying fund positions carry manager-reported marks and performance. Valuations and Look-through show position IRR, TVPI, DPI and RVPI, with support for late manager statements and the resulting restatement of reported values.

- Look-through shows the underlying company holdings behind fund investments. It supports as-of views, shared ownership subtrees and CSV export. Company holdings supplied by import become part of the portfolio's underlying exposure view.

## Questions

### Can I record dated marks for different investment types?

Yes. Valuations supports underlying fund NAV statements, portfolio company valuations and instrument prices. Fund statements record their as-of date and source. Company valuations record the valuation date, method and memo.

### Can a company exit calculation include liquidation preferences?

Yes. Company exit waterfalls calculate proceeds using liquidation preferences, participation and caps. The supported exit action covers the whole holding and allocates the exit value across the company's security classes.

### Can I record late manager marks and underlying-fund performance?

Yes. Underlying fund positions retain manager marks and position performance, including IRR, TVPI, DPI and RVPI. The valuation process supports late quarter statements and restatement of the affected reported values.

### Can I look through fund investments to the underlying companies?

Yes. Look-through presents company holdings behind underlying funds, including shared ownership subtrees and as-of views. Underlying holdings arrive by CSV import, and the look-through results can be exported as CSV.

### Can I prepare the financial statements for one fund and period?

Yes. Fund Statements includes the income statement, balance sheet, statement of changes in net assets and cash-flow statement. These reports use one fund and reporting period for the financial statement set.
