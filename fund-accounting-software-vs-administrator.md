# 1494 Labs and an outsourced fund administrator

Compare the accounting system with the service relationship, and decide which book and reconciliation your team needs to own.

Updated November 3, 2026

## Separate the service decision from the book

A fund administrator provides people and contracted services. Fund accounting software provides the system in which a team keeps and reviews the book. Scope the work first: who books transactions, calculates NAV, reviews allocations, produces investor reports and approves the close?

1494 Labs is fund accounting software for fund teams and administrators. A software demonstration should prove the accounting workflow; a service evaluation should establish responsibility, staffing, reporting deadlines and review arrangements.

## Three workflows to evaluate

- **An internal book.** Your team runs the ledger and close. Confirm that you have the people and review process for the work you retain.

- **A shadow book.** Your team maintains an independent record and compares it with the administrator's reports. Agree the data exchange, timing and break resolution.

- **An administrator-operated book.** Ask what access, exports and supporting records your team receives, and who approves adjustments.

## Test the handoff with one period

1. List the inputs each party supplies and their delivery dates.

2. Reconcile investment positions, cash and opening investor capital.

3. Compare the fee and allocation calculations under the same agreement terms.

4. Record each difference with its source, owner and resolution.

5. Agree which reports are approved and how corrections reach both teams.

1494 Labs includes shadow reconciliation for comparing an outside administrator's book with your own. Ask to see a break identified and traced to its source. For an administrator evaluating the platform, ask to see the workflow across several client funds.
