# Fund Accounting Glossary

Terms for fund managers, allocators and administrators reviewing investor capital, structures and portfolio cash flows.

## Blocker

A corporation held between investors and investments, with tax accounted for at that entity.

[Blocker Corporation Accounting](https://1494labs.com/blocker-corporation-accounting/)

## AIV

An alternative investment vehicle used alongside a fund to hold an investment.

[Holding Company and Family Office Entity Accounting](https://1494labs.com/holding-company-and-family-office-entity-accounting/)

## SPV

A special purpose vehicle formed to hold a particular investment or group of investments.

[Holding Company and Family Office Entity Accounting](https://1494labs.com/holding-company-and-family-office-entity-accounting/)

## Holdco

A holding company that owns interests in other entities.

[Holding Company and Family Office Entity Accounting](https://1494labs.com/holding-company-and-family-office-entity-accounting/)

## Strip sale

A sale of LP interests across several underlying funds in one transaction.

[Secondaries and Strip Sale Accounting](https://1494labs.com/secondaries-and-strip-sale-accounting/)

## Continuation vehicle

A vehicle that acquires investments from an existing fund in a GP-led secondary transaction.

[Secondaries for Fund of Funds](https://1494labs.com/secondaries-for-fund-of-funds/)

## Equalization

An investor-level adjustment used to allocate incentive fees when investors subscribe at different values.

[Equalization and Series Accounting](https://1494labs.com/equalization-and-series-accounting/)

## Series accounting

Separate subscription series used to track investor entry values and fee calculations.

[Equalization and Series Accounting](https://1494labs.com/equalization-and-series-accounting/)

## High-water mark

The value an investor must recover before further performance becomes subject to an incentive fee under the agreed terms.

[Equalization and Series Accounting](https://1494labs.com/equalization-and-series-accounting/)

## Crystallization

The point when an accrued incentive fee becomes payable under the fund terms.

[Equalization and Series Accounting](https://1494labs.com/equalization-and-series-accounting/)

## Side pocket

An investment pool with participation accounted for separately from the main dealing pool.

[Side Pocket Accounting](https://1494labs.com/side-pocket-accounting/)

## Look-through

A view of the holdings behind an investment in another fund or entity.

[Fund of Funds Accounting](https://1494labs.com/fund-of-funds-accounting/)

## Recallable capital

Distributed capital that the fund may call again under its agreement.

[Capital Call and Distribution Software](https://1494labs.com/capital-call-and-distribution-software/)

## Unfunded commitment

The remaining amount an investor has committed and may still be called to contribute.

[Capital Call and Distribution Software](https://1494labs.com/capital-call-and-distribution-software/)

## Commitment pacing

Planning the timing and size of commitments to underlying funds.

[Liquidity Forecasting and Commitment Pacing](https://1494labs.com/liquidity-forecasting-and-commitment-pacing/)

## Clawback

A requirement to return excess carry under the terms of the fund agreement.

[Waterfall and Carried Interest Accounting](https://1494labs.com/waterfall-and-carried-interest-accounting/)

## Hurdle

A return threshold used in calculating carried interest or incentive fees.

[Waterfall and Carried Interest Accounting](https://1494labs.com/waterfall-and-carried-interest-accounting/)

## Catch-up

A waterfall tier that allocates proceeds to bring the carry recipient to the agreed share.

[Waterfall and Carried Interest Accounting](https://1494labs.com/waterfall-and-carried-interest-accounting/)

## ILPA notice

A capital call or distribution notice using the applicable ILPA reporting format.

[Capital Call and Distribution Software](https://1494labs.com/capital-call-and-distribution-software/)
