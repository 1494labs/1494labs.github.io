Everyone who touches the data

Seven third parties, and one of them only if a customer switches something on. This page names all of them, what reaches each one, and where it runs.

Last reviewed 2026-09-25

Processing customer workspace data

ProviderWhat it doesWhat reaches itWhere
Amazon Web Services Runs the application, the database, object storage and the document renderer Everything in a customer workspace United States
Cloudflare Network edge, TLS termination and denial-of-service protection for all three surfaces, plus the analytics that counts page requests Request metadata: network address, the page asked for, timing. Not the contents of a page Global edge network
PostHog Product analytics for the application: which screens are used, and where errors happen The template of the screen opened (never an id or anything shown on it), the kind of any error, a one-way hash of the user and the workspace, and the browser type. Not the network address: our edge removes it first. No fund, investor, figure, name or email United States

Only when a customer turns it on

ProviderWhat it doesWhat reaches itWhere
Anthropic The AI-assisted features, which are off unless a customer enables them Only the documents a customer chooses to process, and nothing else from the workspace United States

Nothing to do with a customer workspace

These three carry contact and website data. None of them can reach a workspace, and no fund, investor or figure passes through them.

ProviderWhat it doesWhat reaches itWhere
Google Email for our own addresses Anything anyone sends us by email, including an enquiry once it is forwarded on United States
Formspree Handles the enquiry form on the home page What a visitor types into that form, and the network address they send it from. Nothing until they press submit United States
GitHub Serves this website Static pages and images. No customer data of any kind United States

Named here because people ask, though neither is a processor

Spaceship is the domain registrar and holds DNS records. It sees no personal data.

Market data providers are read from, not sent to. When a fund tracks an index, the platform fetches a published series. Nothing about a customer, a fund or an investor leaves in that request.

Changes

If a sub-processor is added or replaced, this page changes first and the date above moves. Customers with an agreement in place are told before the change takes effect, not after.
