Why evaluate it

Three things worth an hour of your time.

Terms are configured, not approximated

A waterfall tier, an equalization method, a high-water mark that prorates on partial redemption: each is set to what the fund’s agreement says and tested against example distributions before it touches a live period.

Every balance traces back

A fee on an investor’s capital account leads back to the accrual that produced it, the schedule that calculated it and the journal entry that posted it. You can review an entry and see which balances move before you commit it.

The book checks itself each morning

Capital accounts against the ledger, allocations against the pool, cash movements against the entries that produced them. Anything that stops tying opens a break naming the fund, the period and the amount. What this does not do is tell you a price was wrong; it establishes that the book is consistent with itself, which is a smaller and more checkable claim. With no customers on the platform yet, it runs against our own reference funds — on the same schedule it would run against yours, with nothing to set up.
