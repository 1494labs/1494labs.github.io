A daily sweep that re-proves the arithmetic on every fund on the platformShipped
Capital accounts against the ledger, allocations against the pool, cash movements against the entries that produced them. With no customers on the platform yet, it runs against our own reference funds - which is what it would do against yours, on the same schedule, with nothing to set up.
Internal breaks: a worklist, ageing, and an accept-with-a-note decisionShipped
A break names the fund, the period and the amount. What the sweep does NOT do is tell you a price was wrong - it proves the book is not lying to itself, which is a smaller claim than checking your numbers against a custodian's.
External breaks: ingesting a counterparty file to check cash, positions and NAV against itRoadmap 2027Q1
The worklist, the ageing and the accept-with-a-note decision are the same for an external break and already exist. What is missing is the ingestion of the bank, custodian or administrator file that raises one. If your cash reconciliation lives in another tool today, it stays there until this ships.

What we do not do

CapabilityStatus
Order and execution managementNo
We are the book of record, not the trading stack. There is no order ticket and there will not be one.
Custody, or moving moneyNo
The platform produces the payment file an operator carries to their banking portal. It holds no rails credentials and initiates no transfers.
Tax return preparation and K-1 filingNo
No K-1 workflow exists anywhere in the product. The book supports a preparer; it does not replace one.
An LP-facing investor portalNo
Statements are produced for an administrator to send. There is no investor login.
Market data and pricing feeds we supplyNo
Bring your own provider. We ship the harness that ingests a feed and will not resell data or imply a license we do not hold.
A third-party security attestationRoadmap 2027Q2
No SOC 2 report today. It is a timing decision rather than an oversight, and it is the first thing that changes when a customer's procurement needs it. Stated in full on the security page.
A native mobile applicationNo
The web app renders and is navigable at phone width. There is no native app and none is planned.
