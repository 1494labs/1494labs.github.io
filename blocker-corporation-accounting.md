# Blocker Corporation Accounting

For fund managers, allocators and administrators accounting for investors through blockers.

## Accounting Capabilities

- Blocker setup records the corporation, its underlying fund or AIV investment and the investors behind it. Federal and state tax rates are configured per blocker. Period tax accrual posts an entity-level expense and tax payable; configured deferred tax covers unrealized gains.

- Fund setup provides a Blocker vehicle type, federal and state tax-rate fields, and a choice to accrue deferred tax on unrealized gains. The blocker's fund page shows its period accrual and tax payable.

- The underlying fund allocates the blocker's share before corporate tax. Investors behind the blocker receive their ownership share after the entity's tax expense. A direct LP and a blocker LP can therefore have the same gross allocation and different net amounts.

- Investor statements show both gross allocation and blocker tax. The current-tax expense and payable are posted in the blocker's ledger, and configured deferred tax is carried as a separate accrual.

- Ownership and exposure reports follow the corporation's investment through the underlying fund or alternative investment vehicle to the holdings. The investors behind the blocker remain part of that ownership chain.

## Terms

Blocker and corporate feeder describe the corporation through which US tax-exempt and non-US investors invest. UBTI means unrelated business taxable income; ECI means effectively connected income. Entity-level tax and deferred tax are recorded on the corporation's books.

## Questions

### Does the product account for blocker corporations?

Yes. A blocker is a separate vehicle with its own configured federal and state rates. Period tax accrual posts expense and tax payable on the blocker's books. Deferred tax on unrealized gains is accrued when configured.

### Can investors behind a blocker see gross allocation and tax?

Yes. The blocker's capital account in the underlying fund carries the pre-tax allocation. Each investor behind the corporation carries a share net of blocker tax. Investor statements show the gross allocation and the blocker tax.

### Can I configure federal, state and deferred tax per blocker?

Yes. Federal and state rates are set for each blocker, and deferred tax is enabled per blocker. The accrual records current tax on allocated taxable income and remeasures configured deferred tax on unrealized gains.

### Can ownership reports look through the blocker to holdings?

Yes. The blocker participates in the ownership chain. Look-through and exposure reports trace the investors' ownership through the corporation and the underlying fund or AIV to the recorded holdings.

### Can I see the tax accrual and payable in the product?

Yes. The blocker's fund page provides the period tax accrual and payable view. The accrual action posts the tax expense and payable on the blocker's books, with current and configured deferred amounts recorded for the selected period.
